MPX Global — Category form fields

Ek category select karo — us category ke product/service form me jo fields aayenge wo dikhenge.

Goods (1–34) Service (35–39) Other (seller picks) * = category-specific fields

Common fields sab categories me aate hain. Goods ko trade fields (MOQ, HS code, origin, supply ability, lead time, packaging) milte hain; Service ko engagement / delivery / timeline (no MOQ). Category-specific fields sirf us category ke hote hain — ye CategoryAttribute se dynamic load honge. Status = seller ka active / inactive toggle (live dikhe ya hide) — approval nahi.
