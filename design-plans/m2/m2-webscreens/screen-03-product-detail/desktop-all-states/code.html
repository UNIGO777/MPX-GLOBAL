Description

Our Combed Cotton Poplin Fabric represents the pinnacle of shirting and lightweight apparel textiles. Crafted from meticulously combed cotton fibers, this 120 GSM fabric offers an exceptional balance of durability and breathability, making it ideal for tropical and temperate climates alike.
